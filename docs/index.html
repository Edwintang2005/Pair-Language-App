Pair

Learn each other's languages, five words at a time

Pair is for two people teaching each other: one learns German, the other learns Chinese. Each session you both get five words to learn from your partner and five to teach them.

Getting started

  1. Install Pair on both phones. Each of you picks the language you're learning.
  2. Open today's session together. Teach your partner their words using the notes in the app, and tap I've been taught this as you learn yours.
  3. Finish the session on both phones, then review your words for a few minutes each day.

Common questions

Our phones are on different sessions

Open Settings (the gear on Today) and use Current session to set both phones to the same number. Your review history isn't affected.

Can I use Pair on my own?

Yes, although it's designed for two. Pick the language you're learning and work through the sessions. The teaching notes and examples are there for every word.

Do our phones sync?

No. Pair has no account and no server, so each phone keeps its own progress. That's why you finish each session on both phones.

How do I add the Lock Screen widget?

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customise, then add Pair. To keep one word there, open it in the app and tap Show on Lock Screen. Otherwise you'll get a different word each day.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that reminders are on in Pair's settings and that notifications are allowed for Pair in your iPhone's Settings app.

I found a mistake in a word

Please let us know! Send the word, the session number and what's wrong.
