Pair

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Pair doesn't collect any of your data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What Pair stores, and where

To work, Pair keeps a small amount of information on your iPhone:

  • which language you're learning and which session you're on
  • the words you've been taught and your review history
  • your reminder settings, and the word you've pinned to the Lock Screen widget

This is stored only on your device, inside Pair's own storage and the space it shares with its widget. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Your phone and your partner's phone each keep their own progress. They don't sync with each other.

What Pair doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No servers: Pair never connects to the internet
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party code that collects data

Notifications and pronunciation

If you turn on reminders, Pair schedules them on your phone using iOS's local notifications. Nothing is sent from a server. Pronunciation uses the text-to-speech voices built into iOS.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They help us fix bugs and don't identify you. You can turn this off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Use Reset all progress in Pair's settings, or delete the app. Either one removes everything Pair has stored.

Children

Pair collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
